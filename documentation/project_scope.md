# Project Scope

## Project Name

Operations Performance MIS

## Business Area

Operations / Back Office / Process Management

## Business Scenario

This project simulates a back-office document processing operation.

Operational records are received and processed by employees working across multiple teams, processes, and shifts.

## Business Objective

The objective is to create an MIS system that allows managers to monitor:

1. Employee productivity
2. Team performance
3. Quality and accuracy
4. SLA achievement
5. Attendance
6. Workload
7. Pending records
8. Backlog
9. Errors and rework

## Dataset

The project will contain approximately 1,500 fictional operational records covering multiple employees, teams, processes, shifts, and dates.

## Main KPIs

- Total Records Received
- Total Records Processed
- Pending Records
- Backlog
- Productivity %
- Quality %
- Error Rate
- Rework Rate
- SLA %
- Attendance %
- Absenteeism %

## Final Outputs

### Excel

- Operations Performance MIS
- Daily Operations MIS

### Power BI

1. Executive Overview
2. Employee Performance
3. Quality & SLA
4. Attendance & Workforce

## Management Use Case

The final MIS should help an operations manager quickly identify:

- Teams falling behind
- Employees with low productivity
- Quality issues
- SLA breaches
- Attendance problems
- Increasing backlog
- Workload imbalances

The system should also provide recommended actions based on identified operational issues.
